Learn MS Excel Course - Offline
Developer: Tariq Ahmed
Last updated: October 23, 2026

Overview

Learn MS Excel Course - Offline ("the App") is an educational application that teaches Microsoft Excel through offline lessons. We respect your privacy. This policy explains what data the App collects and how it is used.

Data Collection

The App works 100% offline. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information:

  • No account required – you can use the App without signing up.
  • No personal data collected – we do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or location.
  • Local storage only – your learning progress, quiz scores, and settings are stored only on your device (using local app storage). This data never leaves your phone.
  • No analytics or tracking – the App does not include analytics SDKs or tracking tools.

Permissions

The App may request the following permissions, used only for the stated purpose:

  • Storage / Media access – only to let you save lesson screenshots and your course certificate to your gallery, when you choose to do so.
  • Notifications – only to send you optional daily learning reminders, if you enable them in Settings. You can turn these off at any time.

Advertising

The App may display ads (Google AdMob). Ad providers may use device identifiers to serve personalized ads according to their own privacy policies. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings.

Children's Privacy

The App is a general educational tool suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
